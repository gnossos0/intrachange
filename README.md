# Intrachange  
**The Reunion of Chess and the I Ching**

> “One whole Age ago, a game of perfect proportions captured the language of creation itself.”  
> Intrachange unites the Western logic of **Chess** with the Eastern wisdom of the **I Ching** into a single symbolic system — part game, part meditation, part revelation.

---

##  Philosophy / Purpose

Intrachange is not only a game but a bridge — a reconciliation between two great mirrors of human consciousness.  
Where **Chess** measures action and mastery in the outer world, the **I Ching** measures transformation within the inner one.  
By merging their principles, Intrachange restores what was divided:  
> The body and spirit of play, united once again in a field of perfect proportion.

The player becomes both strategist and sage — the mover and the moved.

##  Overview

Intrachange is a symbolic game engine and interpretive tool that merges **Chess** and the **I Ching** into a unified pattern of play.  

### Includes:
- A functional **dashboard interface**  
- Integrated **Exoteric / Esoteric move logic**  
- **Hexagram mapping system** for tracking transformations  
- **Dynamic text engine** for the Splash Page and narrative sequences  

---

##  Core Concepts

| Element | Description |
|----------|--------------|
| **Exoteric Moves** | Corresponding (outer) actions based on color and line relationship |
| **Esoteric Moves** | Transformative (inner) actions when correspondence breaks |
| **Higher Levels of Play** | Symbolic and archetypal extensions of the core rule system |
| **The Pattern** | 64 Squares ↔ 64 Hexagrams — the unified field of play |
