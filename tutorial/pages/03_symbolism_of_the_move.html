Symbolism of the Move

The true product of Intrachange is not victory or defeat, but the symbolic record of each Move — a reflection of the Player's heart and mind.

This is the lasting treasure preserved in the Archive.

Many Players discover that selecting a theme for their Game beforehand sharpens focus and guides the symbolic narrative that unfolds, move by move.

This game was themed around Star Trek episodes.

As the game continues, players can scroll through the Game History to review posts and follow links.

Now that you appreciate the meaning behind each Move, the next lesson explores the mental skill that allows you to hold multiple layers of significance at once.
