Symbolism of the Move

The true product of Intrachange is not victory or defeat, but the symbolic record of each Move — a reflection of the Player's heart and mind. This is the lasting treasure preserved in the Archive.

Among all elements of the Game, none is more significant than the Player's symbolic record. The Symbolism of the Move is the sole enduring product of play.

Many Players discover that selecting a theme for their Game beforehand sharpens focus and guides the symbolic narrative that unfolds, move by move.

Now that you appreciate the meaning behind each Move, the next lesson explores the mental skill that allows you to hold multiple layers of significance at once.
