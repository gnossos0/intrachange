Archives and Orders

All Games are recorded automatically, allowing Players to review the archives of others. Past Games, played in secrecy, remain protected — a matter of utmost importance to current and former Masters.

The Game is ancient and has spawned numerous Orders. Ours is the oldest and largest, distinguished by the Three Levels: Game, Self-Realization, and Artistic-Creative.

Although all Orders share the I Ching–Chess heritage and Esoteric Moves, other Orders interpret and prioritize differently:

  • Gamut Monks: Use Pieces as a "computer" for projecting future outcomes.
  • New Moon School: Focus on magic, power, and shamanic faculties.
  • Illuminati: Attempted to harness the Game's forces to govern humanity.

All Orders share a common purpose: engaging in the perpetual exchange of ideas in pursuit of deeper insight.
