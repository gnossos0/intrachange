4 :: 4 — Enantiodromia

Transformation Through Opposition

This is the confluence of correlations that creates moves within Chess that are parallel to the line changes within the I Ching.

As energy flows between the system's Dipoles, it does not simply move; it transforms. This process — Enantiodromia — is the phenomenon of one thing turning into its opposite. Within Intrachange, this is the mechanism by which the very identity of a piece, in relation to its square, can provoke a shift in position.

In the I Ching, lines formed by a 7 or 8 are stable, while those formed by a 6 or 9 are changing lines that transform into their opposites. This cycle of transformation is the principle behind Esoteric Moves in Intrachange.
