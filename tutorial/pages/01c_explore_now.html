The Core Concept

The structures of the I Ching and Chess are identical; each enriches the other:

  • The squares, pieces, and moves of Chess embody the hexagrams, lines, and changes of the I Ching.
  • The hexagrams, lines, and changes of the I Ching imbue the squares, pieces, and moves of Chess with meaning.

Chess spatializes the I Ching; the I Ching spiritualizes Chess.

This fusion is what gives Intrachange its mystery and spontaneity. The esoteric moves aren't just a feature — they're the soul of the Game.

Now you know how to begin exploring the Game, and you can return to this tutorial when curiosity brings you back.
