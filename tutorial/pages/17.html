Notes

Because the Rook moves horizontally as well as vertically on the board, it might be expected that its pattern of Esoteric Moves would prove especially powerful but such is not the case; this is because the Rook must, obviously, already occupy an Exoteric Square of the horizontal rank in order to move horizontally—a fact that restricts the Rook's Esoteric Moves to those resulting from vertical Exoteric Moves alone. It is generally a more effective defensive force than an offensive one. It is especially powerless as a threat to the opponent's King so long as that King occupies its home rank, since a Rook's Exoteric Move into the 8th rank results in an Esoteric Move back to its 3rd rank.
