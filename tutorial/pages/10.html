6 :: 6 — Agents in Motion

Stages

Each hexagram in the I Ching is composed of six horizontal lines, stacked vertically to form a complete structure. In Intrachange, this structure finds its direct analogue in the six distinct chess pieces. Each line is a stage of action, and each piece embodies the dynamic qualities of its corresponding line.
