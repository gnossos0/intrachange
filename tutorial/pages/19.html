Notes

Because the Bishop moves backward and forward diagonally in an uninterrupted line, its capacity to act both offensively and defensively is greatly enhanced by its possible Esoteric Moves. Particularly striking are cross-board Exoteric Moves to the King Knight and King Rook ranks for the White Bishop or Queen Knight and Queen Rook ranks for the Black Bishop, all of which result in Esoteric Moves seven horizontal squares away.
