Ranks and Trigrams

The eight I Ching trigrams map to the eight columns and rows of the Intrachange board. Each column carries the energy of its top-trigram archetype, and each row follows the bottom-trigram pattern, creating a lattice of systemic energy.

These eight Types are dynamic exchanges of energy, marking the transitional relationships between states. The intersection of files and rows gives each square a unique energetic signature, guiding piece movement and shaping the flow of the Game.
