Compatibility with Traditional Chess

All rules of traditional Chess apply except as modified in these rules.

Exoteric and Esoteric Moves

  • Every move begins with a legal Chess move, called the Exoteric Square.
  • Half of all Exoteric Moves result in an Esoteric Move, which automatically transfers the Piece to its Esoteric Square.
  • A Piece cannot move into a Square occupied by a Piece of its own color.
