Compatibility with Traditional Chess

All rules of traditional Chess apply except as modified in these rules.

Exoteric and Esoteric Moves

  • Every move begins with a legal Chess move, called the Exoteric Square.
  • Half of all Exoteric Moves result in an Esoteric Move, which automatically transfers the Piece to its Esoteric Square.
  • A Piece cannot move into a Square occupied by a Piece of its own color.

A Note on Automation

Every Esoteric Move in Intrachange is calculated automatically by the software. In the original physical game, Players consulted charts to determine whether a Move was Exoteric or Esoteric and, if necessary, to locate the destination Square. Automating this process preserves the mathematics of the Game while allowing the Player to focus on its strategy and symbolism. You’re welcome.

To learn more about Esoteric Moves, see the Master Level: Patterns of Esoteric Moves.
