1 :: 1 — Identity

Chess :: I Ching

Because the structure of the I Ching and the structure of Chess are identical, Esoteric Chess rests on two principal features:

  1. Chess gives form and substance to the Hexagrams and Line Changes of the I Ching.
  2. The Hexagrams, and Line Changes of the I Ching give meaning and value to Chess.

The two systems interlock with a natural coherence that feels remembered rather than invented.

In other words, the synergism of the Game results from

Chess spatializing the I Ching, and the I Ching spiritualizing Chess.
