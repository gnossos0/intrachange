Modification of Chess Rules

Capture

  • Opponent's Pieces are captured if they occupy the Esoteric Square your Piece moves into.
  • Passing through an Exoteric Square does not capture a piece.

End of Game

  • No Check or Checkmate. The Game ends only when a King is captured by an opponent's Esoteric Move.

Knight and Pawn Exceptions

  • Knights cannot perform Esoteric Moves if any Piece blocks their path.
  • Pawns capture via diagonal Exoteric Move into the 7th rank, then complete an Esoteric Move. Pawns return to the 1st rank after reaching the 7th rank; there is no promotion.
