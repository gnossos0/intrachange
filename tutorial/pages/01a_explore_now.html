Understanding the Game

Page 1 of 3

For most new Players, the most striking thing about Intrachange is how the I Ching–Chess correlation transforms the familiar rules of chess.

Don't worry — you don't need to grasp all the mechanics immediately.

The game only allows legal moves, and most complex operations happen automatically.

You'll learn as you play.

Legal moves are highlighted in green, and if there's a resulting esoteric move, it's highlighted in orange.

After moving, you'll see this prompt asking for the Symbolism of the Move.
