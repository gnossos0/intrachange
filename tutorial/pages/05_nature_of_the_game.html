Nature of the Game

Intrachange is not merely a game; it is a living system where space, time, and consciousness converge. It preserves the cosmological insights of our prehistoric forebears, whose observations of natural forces mirrored those shaping human nature. These forces manifest as rules, patterns, and correspondences governing every move, line, and hexagram on the board.

Esoteric Chess crystallizes this insight — a microcosm of the world encoded in squares, pieces, and moves. Its structure mirrors the I Ching, and its movement enacts it. The squares, pieces, and moves of Chess give form to the hexagrams, lines, and changes of the I Ching; the hexagrams, lines, and changes of the I Ching give meaning and value to the squares, pieces, and moves of Chess.

Some have called it a philosophical sandbox.
