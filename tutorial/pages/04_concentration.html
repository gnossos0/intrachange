Concentration

Intrachange contains few formal exercises, yet the Game trains a singular skill: concentration. Holding two or three keywords in mind simultaneously — and letting their meanings resonate across the board, pieces, hexagrams, and previous moves — is chordal thinking: perceiving multiple points of meaning simultaneously, like hearing several notes combine into a harmonious chord.

In a world overflowing with distraction, the Game offers a rare pause. It invites Players to focus, reflect, and generate significance in the midst of the ordinary. Concentration here is not strain; it is alignment — gathering attention into a coherent field where thought, intuition, and symbolism converge.

Master-level Players recognize that cultivating this capacity is among the Game's deepest rewards. They leverage I Ching interpretations, Correlations, and Keywords to shape the full meaning of their Moves, transforming every action into an orchestration of insight and intention.

With concentration honed, you are ready to perceive the deeper architecture of the Game itself.
