4 :: 4 — Enantiodromia

Hover a selected piece over the board to reveal its hidden esoteric moves.

Underlying PrincipleAs energy flows between the system's Dipoles, it does not simply move; it transforms. This process — Enantiodromia — is the phenomenon of one thing turning into its opposite. Within Intrachange, this is the mechanism by which the very identity of a piece, in relation to its square, can provoke a shift in position.
