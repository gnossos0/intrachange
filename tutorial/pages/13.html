4 :: 4 — Enantiodromia

Each square, like each hexagram line, carries an energetic quality — positive or negative, solid or broken, Black or White.

When a piece correlates perfectly with its square, it remains stable, participating in the steady flow of the system: an Exoteric Move.

But when a piece does not correlate — a mismatch between the piece's polarity and the square's energetic signature — the system imposes a subtle repulsive force, compelling the piece to seek a more harmonious locus.
