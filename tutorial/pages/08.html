2 :: 2 — Duality

Yin :: Yang — Black :: White

At the root of the system is a single polarity expressed two ways.

In the I Ching, Yang is the solid line and Yin is the broken line.
In Chess, White embodies Yang and Black embodies Yin.

The correspondence is one-to-one:

  • Yang ↔ White ↔ Solid line
  • Yin ↔ Black ↔ Broken line

This duality energizes the whole Game and underlies every square, every piece, and every change.
