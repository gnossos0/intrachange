Correlation 4::4

Pattern Recognition

Recognizing the Pattern,

The 4::4 correlation manifests in specific board configurations that the trained eye can identify instantly. Look for these key indicators:

Visual Markers

Pieces positioned on squares where both hexagrams share line 4 as their point of transformation or balance.

Keyword Resonance

When the hexagram keywords of correlated squares share thematic connections related to balance, transition, or mutual influence.

Positional Symmetry

Geometric relationships on the board that mirror the I Ching principle of balanced opposition inherent in line 4.
