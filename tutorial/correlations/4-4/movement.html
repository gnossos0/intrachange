Correlation 4::4

Movement Dynamics

Movement in Correlation,

When pieces share line 4 of their respective hexagrams, they create a resonant connection that influences movement potential. This correlation manifests in three primary ways:

Sympathetic Movement

Pieces in 4::4 correlation may influence each other's movement options, creating opportunities for coordinated positioning.

Resonant Timing

The correlation suggests optimal timing for sequential moves, where one piece's action enhances the other's potential.

Transformation Potential

Line 4 represents a point of balance and transition, making correlated moves particularly powerful for board transformation.
