Correlation 4::4

Introduction to Pattern Recognition

Master Player,

The Correlation 4::4 represents one of the most fundamental pattern relationships in our Game. When two pieces occupy squares that share the same line number in their respective hexagrams, a correlation emerges that transcends the visible board.

This tutorial module will guide you through:

  • Understanding the 4::4 correlation pattern
  • Recognizing movement implications
  • Identifying correlation opportunities
  • Practical application examples

Prepare to see the Game through new eyes.
